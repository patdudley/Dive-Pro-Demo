Privacy & analytics choices

Updated November 11, 2026.

Optional measurement

Dive Pro uses Google Analytics only after you allow analytics in this browser. Analytics helps us compare article visits, reading engagement, onward conditions-page opens and return visits. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives browser/device and usage information. Our added events use page paths and fixed action labels, not feedback messages, search text, email addresses or phone numbers.

You can decline or change your choice with the Privacy & analytics choices button on any measured page. Declining does not disable forecasts, maps or articles. We also keep analytics off when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. Your choice is saved locally in this browser; it does not follow you to another browser or device. Turning analytics off stops future collection here; it does not erase previously collected data.

Other site services

Maps, weather forecasts, hosted camera images and external links can involve other services when you use those features. Optional analytics controls do not disable those features or set preferences on those other sites.
