La Jolla conditions

La Jolla visibility today: a D, 5 to 9 feet, and the rain advisory is still on

The beta model calls Wednesday a D, medium confidence. The county rain advisory is still on this morning. Anticipated lift is still Friday, Nov 20.

18 November 2026 · Dive Pro

The rain part

The countywide general rain advisory is still on this morning. This is from the San Diego County DEHQ feed as I could check it at about 9:25 a.m. Pacific Wednesday, Nov 18. It still listed the advisory on the La Jolla stations, including the Cove, Children's Pool, Avenida De La Playa, Vallecitos, the Scripps Pier stations, and the rest of that La Jolla list. The anticipated lift is still Friday, Nov 20 around 8 a.m. That date is an estimate and it can move. Separate from the rain advisory, the Cove, Children's Pool and End of Marine St also have their own bacteria advisories that were up before this storm. The county's guidance is to stay out of the water during rain and for 72 hours after. Check sdbeachinfo.com (or 619-338-2073) before you go.

The short version

Wednesday, Nov 18. Dive Pro's beta model has La Jolla at a D, 5 to 9 feet of visibility, middle of that range about 7 feet, medium confidence, from the forecast issued at 8:21 a.m. Pacific. D is the biggest bucket but it is close to a three way split, D about 33 percent, C about 32 percent, F about 22 percent, B about 13 percent. A is at 0 percent. Odds, not a promise. A forecast, not a measured report.

Why it's mid

The primary swell is about 3.1 feet at 9 seconds from the W (272 degrees), with a secondary about 2.1 feet at 11 seconds from the WNW. Surf height is about 5.3 feet this morning and eases to about 3.1 feet by late evening. Yesterday's wave height was about 7.2 feet, so it is smaller today. The model still says elevated surf can stir the shallows. Rain in the prior 3 days was about 0.45 inches, and none is forecast today. Nearshore water is about 74 degrees. The Scripps nearshore buoy is at 74.3. The model's best window is early to late morning before the wind builds.

Wind and tide

Wind is light. Mostly 3 to 7 mph all day, max about 7, gusts to about 13 mph, from the WNW (291 degrees). The tide, from NOAA La Jolla 9410230, was falling at the 8:21 a.m. issuance to a low of about 2.4 feet at 10:44 a.m. Then a high of about 3.7 feet near 4 p.m. (15:56), and a low around 1 foot near 10:27 p.m. The early morning high, about 4.2 feet near 4:55 a.m., already passed. Tide height alone doesn't tell you what the water looks like. Use it for timing.

Bottom line

A D, 5 to 9 feet, smaller surf than yesterday, light wind, and the rain advisory is still on. The county's lift estimate is still Friday, Nov 20 around 8 a.m., and that can move. If you go anyway, 5 to 9 feet is the model's range, and think twice about putting your face in runoff. Full numbers are on the La Jolla forecast page. Dive Pro's model is beta and not validated.
