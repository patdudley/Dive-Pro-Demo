La Jolla Visibility Today: C Grade, 10 to 14 Feet, and the Rain Advisory Is Still On

La Jolla visibility for Friday, Nov 20, 2026: Dive Pro's beta model says C grade, 10 to 14 feet. Short-period west swell, calm morning, and a county rain advisory through the weekend.

20 November 2026 · Dive Pro

The short version

Friday, Nov 20. Sitting in the Cove lot. Dive Pro's beta model says C. The estimated range is 10 to 14 feet. Medium confidence. Generated at 8:01 AM PT. This is a forecast, not a measurement. The best window on the model is early to late morning, before the wind builds.

Why it's mid

Swell is from the west, 267 degrees. About 3.8 feet at a short 5.4 second period. Surf is around 5.6 feet. Short period means it is bumpy, and it stirs the shallows. That is why the model flags surface movement. Wind chop adds about 2 feet. You get that extra bump on the surface even while the morning air is still quiet. Water temp is about 74 F on the NDBC 46254 buoy.

Wind and tide

Wind is about 4 mph through 10 AM. Then 11 mph by 11 AM. Afternoon is 12 to 15 mph, gusts up to about 21 mph. Morning is the window. Show up after 11 and you are in the windier half of the day.

Tide was high around 5:38 AM at about 5 feet. It falls to a low of about 1 foot at 12:15 PM, then back up to a high near 6 PM. Tide alone does not set visibility. Use it to time the entry.

The rain part

It rained Thursday, Nov 19. About 0.64 inches over the past 7 days. The San Diego County Department of Environmental Health and Quality has a General Rain Advisory covering La Jolla beaches. That includes the Cove, Children's Pool, the Windansea area (Palomar), and the Scripps Pier area. The county says avoid swimming, surfing, and diving for 72 hours after rain. The estimated lift is Sunday, Nov 22, around 5:00 PM PT. That time is subject to change. It is not a guarantee. The source is San Diego County beach water quality (sandiegocounty.gov, sdbeachinfo).

Runoff near storm drains and outlets is the concern. The visibility grade says nothing about bacteria or entry safety. That C is how far Dive Pro's beta model thinks you can see. It does not clear you to get in. La Jolla Cove also carries a separate older advisory. It has been posted since late September, and it is still up.

Bottom line

Honest take. This is a mid day. Go early if you go. Check the county page yourself. Full numbers are on the La Jolla forecast page.
