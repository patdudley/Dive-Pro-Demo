Tuesday morning at the Cove, the water is still high on the rocks.

La Jolla visibility today is a mid C. The beta model has Tuesday at C grade, 10 to 14 feet, medium confidence. That Tuesday line is the latest published forecast, stamped Monday, Nov 9, at 3:13 p.m. Pacific. No newer run is in the file. C is the middle letter. Ten to fourteen feet means you can dive it. A dozen feet is the middle of that range. Medium confidence means the guess can miss.

Surf sits at 3.81 feet, the same Open-Meteo number as Monday in this run. The main swell is 3.15 feet out of the west at 10.55 seconds. Under it sits 1.77 feet of south-southwest swell at 12.6 seconds. Period is the seconds between waves. Monday's second swell was west-northwest, 1.71 feet at 13.8 seconds. Today that second swell has south in it. The model still flags the surf as enough to stir the shallows. It still calls the longer period a plus, easier to time than short chop.

The odds are wide. About 42 percent on the C. About 35 percent on a B. About 16 percent on a D. About 7 percent on an F. Zero on an A. Those are the model's own odds. Mid is mid. It is not a lock.

Tide range is 6.33 feet. Range is the drop from the high sample to the low sample.
