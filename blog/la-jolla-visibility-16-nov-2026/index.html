La Jolla visibility today: a D, 5 to 9 feet, rain advisory about to lift

The beta model calls Monday a D with low confidence. The county rain advisory for La Jolla is expected to lift Tuesday, Nov 17 around 8 a.m.

16 November 2026 · Dive Pro

The short version

Monday, Nov 16. Dive Pro's beta model has La Jolla at a D, 5 to 9 feet of visibility, low confidence. That's from the forecast issued Sunday at 3:28 p.m. Pacific. Today's refresh had not published yet. Low confidence means the model isn't sure. Its biggest bucket is actually a C at about 41 percent, F at about 31 percent, and D at about 23 percent, so the odds are spread out. Odds, not a promise. A forecast, not a measured report.

Why it's mid

The swell eased. It is about 4.3 feet at 11 seconds from the west, with a second swell near 2.3 feet at 11 seconds under it. Sunday it was about 6 feet, so the surf is coming down, which helps. It is still enough to stir the shallows. The model's best window is early to late morning.

Wind and tide

Morning is calm, mostly under 8 mph until about 10 a.m. Then it comes up to around 12 to 13 mph midday and afternoon, with gusts up to about 25 mph, and it pushes harder tonight. The tide is rising to a high of about 4 feet around 1 p.m., then dropping to a low near 9 p.m. at under 1 foot. Tide height alone doesn't tell you what the water looks like. Use it for timing.

The rain part

The county's general rain advisory for San Diego coastal waters, including the La Jolla stations, has been up since the Nov 13 rain. It was extended after more rain on Nov 14. The guidance is to avoid swimming, surfing and diving for 72 hours after rain. The anticipated lift is Tuesday, Nov 17 around 8 a.m., and that can change. Check sdbeachinfo.com before you go. The model inputs show about 0.19 inch of rain over the prior three days, and a little more, about 0.08 inch, in today's forecast.

Bottom line

Mid water, easing swell, advisory still on until at least Tuesday morning. If you go anyway, 5 to 9 feet is the model's range, and think twice about putting your face in runoff. Full numbers are on the La Jolla forecast page. Dive Pro's model is beta and not validated.
