La Jolla Visibility Today: C Grade, Surf Stepped Up

La Jolla visibility for Monday, Nov 9, 2026: published beta forecast reads C grade, 10 to 14 feet. Surf near 3.8 feet, a 6.5 foot tide swing to a minus low, and about a 45 percent chance the letter holds.

9 November 2026 · Dive Pro

Monday at the Cove, the water is still high on the rocks and already headed down.

La Jolla visibility today is a mid C. Our beta model has Monday at C grade, 10 to 14 feet, medium confidence. The run is from 9:23 a.m. Pacific. C is the middle letter. Ten to fourteen feet means you can dive it. A dozen feet sits in the middle of that range. Medium confidence means that guess can miss.

Surf sits around 3.8 feet, up from Sunday's 3.4. The main swell is about 3.2 feet out of the west-northwest at about 10.9 seconds. Under it is a 1.7 foot west-northwest swell at 13.8 seconds. Period is the seconds between waves. The model flags that surf as enough to stir the shallows. It also calls the longer period a plus, easier to time than short chop.

The odds are wide. About 45 percent on the C. About 27 percent on a B. About 15 percent on a D. About 13 percent on an F. Those numbers are the model's own odds. A miss can go up or down.

Tide range is about 6.5 feet. Range is the drop from high water to low water. High sits near 6 feet around 8:07 a.m. Low is around 3:18 p.m., about minus 0.46 feet. The next high is about 3.7 feet at 9:41 p.m. The model counts the rise into that high as a plus, because incoming water can bring cleaner water in from offshore. It is after dark. The published window is early to late morning, before the wind builds.
