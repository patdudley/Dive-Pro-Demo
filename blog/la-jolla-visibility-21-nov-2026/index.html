La Jolla Visibility Today: C Grade, 10 to 14 Feet, and the Rain Advisory Is Still On

La Jolla visibility for Saturday, Nov 21, 2026: Dive Pro's beta model says C grade, 10 to 14 feet. Short period west swell, a light morning, and the county rain advisory is still on.

21 November 2026 · Dive Pro

The short version

Saturday, Nov 21. Sitting in the Cove lot. Dive Pro's beta model says C. The estimated range is 10 to 14 feet. Medium confidence. Generated at 7:15 AM PT. This is a forecast, not a measurement. The model is not sure. Grade odds are C 42%, D 29%, B 16%, and F 13%. Those odds are not a lock. C is the call. D is the next most likely. The best window is early to late morning, before the wind builds.

Why it's mid

The main swell is from the west, 268 degrees. About 3.3 feet at a short 5.2 second period. A second, longer swell is from the WNW, 286 degrees. That one is about 2.1 feet at a 10 second period. Surf is about 4.7 feet, down from about 5.4 feet yesterday. The short period swell stirs the shallows. Wind chop is only about half a foot. Water is about 74 F on the NDBC 46254 buoy. Air is 49 to 67 F.

Wind and tide

Wind is light. Roughly 3 to 6 mph through late morning. Afternoon is 7 to 10 mph, gusts up to about 15 mph. Morning is the window.

Tide is from NOAA La Jolla, station 9410230. High near 6:05 AM, about 5.6 feet. It falls to a low near 12:56 PM, about 0.2 feet, then back up to a high near 7 PM, about 3.8 feet. Tide alone does not set visibility. Use it to time the entry.

The rain part

About 0.5 inches of rain fell over the past 7 days. None is forecast today. A dry morning does not lift the advisory. The San Diego County Department of Environmental Health and Quality still has a General Rain Advisory on La Jolla beaches. That covers the Cove, Children's Pool, the Windansea area, La Jolla Shores, and the Scripps Pier area. The county says avoid swimming, surfing, and diving for 72 hours after rain. The estimated lift is Sunday, Nov 22, around 5:00 PM PT. That time can change. It is an estimate, not a guarantee. The source is San Diego County beach water quality (sdbeachinfo).

La Jolla Cove also carries a separate older advisory. It has been posted since late September, and it is still up. The visibility grade says nothing about bacteria or entry safety. That C is how far Dive Pro's beta model thinks you can see. It does not clear you to get in.

Bottom line

Honest take. This is a mid day. Go early if you go. Check the county page yourself. Full numbers are on the La Jolla forecast page.
