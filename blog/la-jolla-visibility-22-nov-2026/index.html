La Jolla Visibility Today: C Grade, 10 to 14 Feet, and a Small South Swell

La Jolla visibility for Sunday, Nov 22, 2026: Dive Pro's beta model says C grade, 10 to 14 feet. A small long-period south swell, light wind, and the county rain advisory set to lift this evening.

22 November 2026 · Dive Pro

The short version

Sunday, Nov 22. Standing in the Cove lot. Dive Pro's beta model says C. The estimated range is 10 to 14 feet. Medium confidence. Generated at 7:12 AM PT. This is a forecast, not a measurement. Grade odds are C 53%, B 29%, F 10%, and D 8%. So C is the call, with a real shot at B. Not a lock. The model also puts a little weight on a bad day.

The swell is small and from the south

Yesterday was a short period west swell. Today is different. The main swell is from the SSW, 197 degrees. About 2.1 feet at 14 seconds. A second SSW pulse is about 1.5 feet at 11 seconds. Surf is about 2.9 feet. That is down from about 3.8 feet yesterday and about 5.5 feet the day before. Wind chop is basically nothing, about a tenth of a foot. Less going on in the water is the reason the model is not scared of today.

Wind and tide

Wind is light all day. Around 6 to 7 mph early, dropping to about 3 to 4 mph by 10 and 11. Gusts top out near 13 mph. The model flags afternoon wind as a small risk, and the numbers back that up as a light one. Water is about 74 F on the NDBC 46254 buoy. Air is roughly 50 to 69 F.

Tide is from NOAA La Jolla, station 9410230. High near 6:35 AM, about 6 feet. It drains to a low near 1:38 PM, about minus 0.5 feet. Then back up to a high near 7:50 PM, about 3.8 feet. Tide alone does not set visibility. It tells you when the water is moving.

The rain part

No rain today. The last rain event on the county's books is Nov 19. The San Diego County Department of Environmental Health and Quality still has a General Rain Advisory up across La Jolla. That includes the Cove, Children's Pool, Windansea, and La Jolla Shores. The county's anticipated lift is Nov 22 at about 5:00 PM. Subject to change. An estimate, not a guarantee. Check sdbeachinfo before you go.

La Jolla Cove also has its own older advisory, posted since late September. A visibility grade says nothing about bacteria or entry safety. C is how far the beta model thinks you can see.

Bottom line

Decent odds of a usable day on paper. If the advisory matters to you, the clock says evening. Full numbers are on the La Jolla forecast page.
