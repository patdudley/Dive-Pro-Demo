Thursday morning at the Cove, and the newest number is still Wednesday's.

La Jolla visibility today is a C. The beta model has C grade, 10 to 14 feet, medium confidence. That run is stamped Wednesday, Nov 11, at 1:00 p.m. Pacific. No Thursday run is published yet. C is the middle letter and a dozen feet is the middle of the range. Medium confidence means it can miss.

The odds are spread out. About 48 percent on the C. About 36 percent on a B. About 16 percent on a D. Zero on an A or an F. Those are the model's own odds, so think of it as mid with a shot at better, not a lock.

Surf in the file is 3.7 feet. The main swell is 2.9 feet out of the west at 9 seconds, with 1.8 feet of south-southwest swell at 11.7 seconds underneath. Period is the seconds between waves. The model says that is enough surf to stir up the shallows, but the overall swell load is modest.

Wind is light. It runs about 3 to 4 mph through the morning and creeps up to around 7 mph by mid afternoon, gusts topping out near 11 mph. The model likes the early window before the wind builds.

The file's tide numbers are Wednesday's: high at 11:15 p.m. after a low near 4:30 p.m. It has no Thursday tide times, so check a tide table for your own window.
